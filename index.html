A progress meeting
Every week

Before we start, we agree on the scope, the price, and the paperwork. Once work begins, we meet every week to show you progress and hear your feedback.

You see the work as it develops, with a regular chance to ask questions and discuss changes.

  1. Start with a call

    Email us your idea. We’ll schedule a discovery call to understand what you need and what you want to achieve.

  2. Agree on the work

    We send a quote based on that conversation. Once we agree on the scope and price, we handle the paperwork and get started.

  3. Review it together

    Weekly meetings give you a look at the work in progress. We review it together and discuss your feedback as we go.
